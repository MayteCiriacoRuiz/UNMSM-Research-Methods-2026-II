## Reproducibility notes

- Database: Scopus, searched on 24 October 2026 in three iterations (0, 72 and 44 records). The third is the final strategy.
- Criteria v1.0 (I1–I6, tiers, tie-break, exclusion codes E1–E9) were written before screening decisions.
- An AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) was the primary screener at title and abstract level; the human reviewer validated a random sample (seed 20261024) plus one low-confidence record, six of 44 records in total.
- Declared deviation: full texts were assessed in a fixed priority order and reading stopped once ten eligible studies were confirmed; fifteen records are reported as E9 (eligible by abstract, not assessed).
- Passages for records 30 and 9 were checked against the PDFs; the other quotations come from abstracts. See the Limitations section of the deliverable.
